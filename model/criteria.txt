SCREENING CRITERIA
Topic: solar sails in asteroid systems

Review question:
What attitude control methods are used to stabilize the orbit of solar sails in asteroid systems?

1.INCLUDE (label = 1) if the paper:
1.1.proposes, analyzes, or simulates a solar sail mission to an asteroid or asteroid system (rendezvous, orbiting, landing, deflection, mining, etc.)
1.2.studies the orbital dynamics or control of a solar sail in the vicinity of an asteroid, including binary asteroids
1.3.proposes a trajectory, guidance, or attitude control method applied to a solar sail with an asteroid as the target or environment
1.4.treats the asteroid as central to the study (target, environment, or mission objective)

2.EXCLUDE (label = 0) if the paper:
2.1.addresses solar sails with no relation to asteroids (Moon, Mars, deep space, planetary missions, etc.)
2.2.addresses asteroids without solar sails (e.g., electric propulsion only)
2.3.mentions solar sails or asteroids only in passing (in the introduction, a list of examples, or future work)
2.4.is only about sail materials or manufacturing, with no application to asteroids
2.5.is not a technical study (editorial, news item, event summary)

3.DOUBTFUL CASES:
3.1.Only the title and abstract are available: decide based on them, without looking up the full paper.
3.2.Solar sail and asteroid appear together, but the main focus is something else: mark 0.
3.3.Magnetic sail, electric sail, or laser sail: mark 0, unless the paper compares it with a sol'ar sail for asteroids. (Adjust according to what you consider relevant.)
